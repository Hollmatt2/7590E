What it does

Requester submitsRead and split into clausesText rules + AI find provisions Reviewer accepts, dismisses or escalatesApprover gives the outcomeHistory recordReports
  • Every finding shows the exact words, highlighted in the clause
  • Reading and the AI run in a separate background program, so submitting never waits
  • The system never decides. It points at clauses; a person makes and signs every call.

How well it works

RatingProvisions
ReliableGoverning law (90% found), auto-renewal, notice period (88% found). The notice period is the clause behind the missed renewal.
Useful with checkingAssignment, change of control, termination for convenience, cap on liability, insurance, audit rights
Not reliableUncapped liability: 30% found, 71% of flags wrong. Kept in and marked, because it is Calder's first problem. Until it improves, Legal reads every liability section.

Modified-agreement test: 10 Bonterms / Common Paper agreements changed on purpose. A reversed assignment clause was still flagged, but the system could not tell it had been reversed. That is why every flag shows the exact words.
